The Ultimate Football Quiz

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains what data The Ultimate Football Quiz uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

The Ultimate Football Quiz (the “app”) is published by Baptiste Aguado, an independent developer based in France (“we”, “us”). For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at baptisteaguado@hotmail.com.

This policy covers the iOS app. We do not run any server, account system or user database for the app: you never create an account and we never receive your name, email or contacts.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game progress stays on your iPhone: levels completed, coins, hints and jokers, statistics and settings. It is stored locally by the app and is never sent to us. Deleting the app deletes this data.

We do not use any analytics tool of our own and we do not sell personal data.

3. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • device and advertising identifiers (on iOS, the advertising identifier only if you allow tracking);
  • IP address and an approximate location derived from it;
  • information about ad interactions, app usage and crashes linked to ads.

Your choices

  • Tracking (iOS): the app asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you refuse, ads are not personalised with your advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): on first launch a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform asks whether you accept personalised ads. Non-personalised ads are shown if you decline. You can change your choice anytime in the app: Settings → Ad choices.
  • No ads: you can remove forced ads with the “No ads” in-app purchase. Optional reward videos you choose to watch remain available.

Google’s use of data is described in its privacy policy and on how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Legal basis (GDPR): your consent for personalised ads; our legitimate interest in funding a free app for non-personalised ads.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (coins, question packs, “No ads”) are processed entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your payment details. To unlock what you bought and restore it on a new device, the app receives a purchase receipt from Apple, handled through RevenueCat, Inc., which stores an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. See Apple’s privacy policy and RevenueCat’s privacy policy.

5. Children, your rights and changes

Children. The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. Ads are configured according to Google’s child-directed and under-age-of-consent settings where they apply.

Your rights. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may ask to access, correct or delete data, object to processing or withdraw consent. As we hold no personal data ourselves, most choices are made in the app (Settings → Ad choices), in iOS Settings, or directly with Google, Apple and RevenueCat. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL).

Retention. Data on your device stays until you delete the app. Data collected by Google, Apple or RevenueCat is kept according to their own policies.

Changes. We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest version.
